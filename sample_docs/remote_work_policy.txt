XYZ Group — Remote Work Policy (v2.3)

1. Eligibility
Employees who have completed 90 days of continuous employment are eligible
to apply for a remote work arrangement. Approval is granted by the
employee's direct manager and must be renewed annually.

2. Work Hours
Remote employees must be reachable during core hours of 10:00-15:00 in
their local time zone. Outside core hours, flexible scheduling is
permitted as long as weekly deliverables are met.

3. Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office stipend of $300
CAD. Employees are responsible for their own internet connectivity.

4. Data Security
Remote employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems
and must not save confidential documents to personal, non-encrypted
devices.

5. Review
Remote work arrangements are reviewed every 6 months by HR and may be
revoked if performance metrics fall below team baseline for two
consecutive quarters.
