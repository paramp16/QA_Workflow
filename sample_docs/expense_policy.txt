XYZ Group — Expense Reimbursement Policy (v1.4)

1. Submission Window
All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date
via the internal Expensely portal. Claims submitted after 30 days require
Finance Director approval.

2. Receipts
Receipts are required for any single expense over $25 CAD. Expenses under
$25 CAD may be self-certified without a receipt, up to a monthly cap of
$100 CAD in self-certified claims.

3. Travel
Economy class flights are standard for trips under 6 hours. Business
class requires VP-level approval and is only available for flights over
6 hours in duration.

4. Meals
Meal reimbursement while travelling on company business is capped at $75
CAD per day, inclusive of tips.

5. Ineligible Expenses
Alcohol, personal entertainment, and traffic/parking fines are not
reimbursable under any circumstances.
